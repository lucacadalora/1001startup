Indonesia computer imports: an illustrative AI factory scenario
Research note dated 23 September 2026

SOURCE AND SCOPE
Two supplied spreadsheet exports, each with one Data worksheet:
- indonesias-imports-from-world-by-product_8471 (3).xlsx: monthly,
  January 2025 through July 2026.
- indonesias-imports-from-world-by-product_8471 (2).xlsx: quarterly,
  Q1 2025 through Q2 2026. Its 202501-202504 and 202601-202602 headers
  represent quarters in this export, as confirmed by monthly reconciliation.

Reporter: 360, Indonesia. Partner: 000, World. Original values: US$ thousands.
These supplied files have not been independently authenticated against the
original issuing data provider. Internal reconciliation checks consistency;
it does not establish the accuracy or provenance of the underlying records.
The downloads here are derived data, not authenticated customs declarations.

monthly.csv retains the monthly HS 8471 total and the selected HS6 categories
847150, 847149 and 847180. categories.csv compares all eight HS6 categories
and the parent 8471 total. Columns marked USDm are US$ millions.
The monthly reconciliation column is in US$ thousands and compares the sum
of all eight source subcategories with the parent total, not only the three
selected subcategories included in monthly.csv.

Do not add the parent HS 8471 row to its component categories. Do not combine
the quarterly and monthly exports as if they were separate imports.
All 54 category-quarter comparisons agree within US$1,000; monthly sums of
the eight subcategories agree with the parent within US$2,000, consistent
with rounding. Comparisons below use January-July in both years.

Contribution to the HS 8471 increase:
(3,941.371 - 1,610.584) / (5,255.474 - 2,773.194) = 93.897%, rounded to 94%.
This is the share of the increase, not an estimate of the AI share.

Processing-unit imports in May, June and July 2026 were respectively
US$654.453 million, US$852.058 million and US$661.632 million.

ILLUSTRATIVE ALLOCATION
Assume 50% of HS 847150 and 35% of HS 847149 are AI computing equipment:
2,850.872 x 0.50 + 1,090.499 x 0.35 = US$1,807.11065 million,
rounded to US$1.8 billion in the note.

The 50% and 35% shares are analyst-selected assumptions. They are not
estimated from shipment descriptions, fitted probabilities, or verified
allocations. These broad categories also cover conventional servers,
workstations and industrial computers. The resulting estimate concerns
equipment value, not GPU-chip value or the total cost of an AI factory.
It does not include a separately estimated amount for power, cooling,
networking or construction.

LIMITS OF THE INFERENCE
These aggregate workbooks contain no importer names, counterparties,
product models, part numbers or installation status. They cannot establish
that any specific Indonesian company imported NVIDIA B300 systems, or that
the equipment is installed or operational. No HS code here is exclusive to
B300. Zero B300 imports remains compatible with this evidence alone.
Company/model attribution requires shipment-level documents specifying
importer, date, model or part number, quantity and declared value.

The earlier social-media chart of Taiwan chip exports is a different series
with an unverified HS filter; it is not used in these calculations.
