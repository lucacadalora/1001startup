2.7 AI answer engines (GEO and AEO)
Facts:

  • ChatGPT used the pages to answer 1,084 times, and Claude 99 times (your screenshot) [E19].
  • The site already has strong GEO parts. Each company page has an answer sentence, a "Kutip sebagai" line,
    dateModified in schema, and /llms.txt is live [E13].
  • Example answer sentence: "Menurut sensus 1001startup.id (diperbarui 1 Okt 2026), Astro tercatat 994
    pekerja (dibaca 2026-08-29); pendanaan yang diumumkan US$142 juta; kantor pusatnya di Jakarta
    Barat." (https://1001startup.id/id/company/astro) [E13].
  • Google says that its AI features need no new machine-readable files, AI text files or markup [E17].
  • Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features, last updated
    2025-12-10 UTC.
  • Google also says there is "no special schema.org structured data that you need to add" (same page) [E17].
  • So llms.txt is optional. Keep it, because it states the citation rules. Do not expect Google rankings from it.

How the pages earn citations:

 1. Put an answer-first block at the top of each hub (home, /investors, /unicorn, /closed, sector, city,
    research).
 2. In that block, use one sentence with the number, the date and the source. Copy the pattern from the
    company template.
 3. Keep the Dataset schema on home.
 4. Add license, temporalCoverage, variableMeasured, distribution and dateModified to the Dataset schema.
 5. Show clear entity facts, one fact per line: legal name, sector, head office, founded year, founders, last
    round and investors.
 6. Make the schema match the visible text.
 7. Show "diperbarui [date]" on each hub, and use the same date in schema dateModified.
 8. Keep the census count in llms.txt current.

Note on step 4: Google lists the first four of these properties as Dataset recommended properties (https://
developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/dataset, last updated 2026-09-08
UTC) [E17]. The company template already has an answer sentence.

3.4 Thin pages and quality gates
Why: the September 2026 spam update was rolling out on 5 Oct 2026 [E20]. Google says: "Scaled content
abuse is when many pages are generated for the primary purpose of manipulating search rankings and not
helping users." (https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies, fetched 5 Oct
2026) [E17]. The census is real, sourced data. But empty templates still look like scaled pages. Put a gate on
them.

Company pages (recommendation):

 1. Index a company page only if 4 of these 7 fields have a source:
      • disclosed funding
      • last round
      • valuation
      • headcount with read date
      • head office address
      • investors (1 or more)
      • founder or founding year

 2. Below 4 fields, set "noindex, follow". Remove the URL from the sitemap.
 3. Count how many of the 387 rows fail the gate [DATA FROM LUCA].

Example: /company/qalboo has no funding or headcount in its meta description (fetched 5 Oct 2026) [E13].
The home page says 208 of 387 rows have disclosed funding [E13].

6.1 Why not a classic PBN or a mass AI blog
Google's spam policies do not use the words "PBN" or "private blog network" [E20]. But they cover the PBN
methods.


      POLICY      Why not a classic PBN or a mass AI blog
       • Google: "Link spam is the practice of creating links to or from a site primarily for the purpose of
         manipulating search rankings." (spam policies, https://developers.google.com/search/docs/
         essentials/spam-policies, last updated 2026-08-28 UTC) [E17].
       • Google lists "Buying or selling links for ranking purposes" as link spam (same page).
       • Google: "Expired domain abuse is where an expired domain name is purchased and repurposed
         primarily to manipulate search rankings by hosting content that provides little to no value to users."
         (same page).
       • Google: "Scaled content abuse is when many pages are generated for the primary purpose of
         manipulating search rankings and not helping users." (same page).
       • Google lists "Creating multiple sites with the intent of hiding the scaled nature of the content" as
         scaled content abuse (same page).
       • Google also lists "Using generative AI tools or other similar tools to generate many pages without
         adding value for users" (same page).
       • So, many AI posts in one day can be scaled content abuse. The definition names the purpose of the
         pages, not a page type. News pages and research pages are not outside it.
       • Google's spam-updates guide says: "In the case of a link spam update (an update that specifically
         deals with link spam), making changes might not generate an improvement. ... Any potential ranking
         benefits generated by those links cannot be regained." (https://developers.google.com/search/
         docs/appearance/spam-updates, last updated 2025-12-10 UTC) [E20].

     My plan uses real related sites with clear brand ties and editorial links. It uses nofollow or sponsored
     where links are not editorial. It adds digital PR from the census data.
