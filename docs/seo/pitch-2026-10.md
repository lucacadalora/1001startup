## English pitch

Subject: Indonesia startup data: 10 companies hold 63% of disclosed funding (census, Oct 2026)

I'm building 1001startup.id, a public census of Indonesian startups where every figure links to the document it comes from. We have just published the October 2026 edition of our report, with the figures frozen so they can be cited: https://1001startup.id/report/2026-10

Four findings from the 438 startups on record:

1. Ten companies hold 62.7% of the US$18.5 billion in disclosed funding (222 of the 438 disclose a figure). Gojek (GoTo Group) alone accounts for US$5.3 billion.
2. 567 investors are named on 338 census companies. East Ventures backs 53 of them, more than any other investor; 420 investors appear on a single company.
3. 35 Indonesian startups stopped operating between 2019 and 2025, 14 of them in 2023. The 16 with a disclosed round had raised US$1.07 billion between them, led by eFishery (US$415 million). Nine of the 35 are reported by credible press but not confirmed by the company, and the page marks which.
4. Only 25 of the 438 carry a public valuation; 10 are at US$1 billion or more (7 from private rounds, 2 stock-market capitalisations, 1 estimate).

Credit line: "Source: 1001startup.id census, October 2026 edition" and a link.
Limitation: since 28 September the census counts companies anywhere in Indonesia, but coverage outside Greater Jakarta is still being built, so the Jakarta share reflects coverage, not a national finding.
