# Product

## Register
brand

## Users and purpose
1001startup.id is a bilingual census of Indonesian startups. Readers browse companies and inspect the sources behind funding, employment and economic estimates. Research adds dated analysis with downloadable evidence and explicit assumptions.

## Brand and principles
Follow the existing census identity: precise, sourced and restrained. Existing news-view documentation specifies Overused Grotesk, Plex Mono, hairlines and no press photos or reference-site cloning. Preserve that identity for Research. Separate observations from estimates; disclose provenance; never invent company or equipment attribution.

## Accessibility and inclusion
Preserve server-rendered English and Indonesian pages, native links and language switching. New data graphics need text equivalents; tables need headers and mobile scrolling. Maintain visible keyboard focus and readable prose without motion.
